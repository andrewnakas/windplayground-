How to read these tables

Every row is scored by the same code on the same init times and truth. The rows fall into four groups:

groupwhat it is
baselinespersistence, climatology, per-pixel ridge regression
ours (from scratch)U-Net / ViT / AFNO / mesh-GNN trained here on 4 CPU cores at 5.625 deg, _ft2/_ft4 = rollout fine-tuned
published frontier forecaststhe actual GraphCast / GenCast / Pangu / HRES forecasts, produced at 0.25 deg and regridded to 64x32 by WeatherBench 2
ours: combinationspost-processing and blends built on top of those published forecasts

Our from-scratch models are not a like-for-like test of the architectures: the frontier models used hundreds of GPU/TPU-days at 0.25 deg. Treat those rows as the reference ceiling. The comparable published anchor at our resolution is Rasp & Thuerey 2021 (ResNet, 5.625 deg): z500 RMSE 268 / 499 m2/s2 at 3/5 days. The blend rows are like-for-like -- they beat those models on their own published forecasts, at the resolution everything here is scored on.
